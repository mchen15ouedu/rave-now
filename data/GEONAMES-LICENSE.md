# City directory attribution

`city-directory.json.gz` contains a compact adaptation of GeoNames' `cities1000.zip`, country names, first-level administrative names, and US postal-code coordinates, downloaded October 5, 2026. Names, country/region codes, coordinates, populations, and IANA time zones were selected from the original records. Some obsolete and city-subdivision records were omitted. US postal codes were indexed by code. This directory contains public place data, never browser users or their locations.

Data © [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is supplied as is, without guarantees of accuracy, completeness, or timeliness. City and postal coordinates represent approximate centers, not verified street addresses or road travel times.

Original downloads and format descriptions: [gazetteer files](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/), [gazetteer format](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt), [US postal codes](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/US.zip), and [postal format](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/readme.txt).
